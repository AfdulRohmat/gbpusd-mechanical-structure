# Technical Plan — Phase 1.2 P3 Light-Filter Ablation

**Status:** preregistered before coverage screening and filter P&L  
**Branch:** `phase/01-2-light-filter-ablation`  
**Parent evidence:** Phase 1.1 fingerprint `90d1e369b427d3d8`

## 1. Research objective

Full-session P3 supplies approximately 32 trades per month but remains negative.
Phase 1.2 asks whether a light, causally available filter can retain 20–25
trades per month while improving signal quality enough to become positive after
full costs.

The study does not reuse the sparse exact-alignment P4 rule. It tests vetoes:
an explicitly opposing higher-timeframe state can reject a setup, while
`transition`, `balance`, `undetermined`, and directionally aligned states remain
eligible.

## 2. Protection against post-hoc selection

London-only, New-York-only, BOS-only, CHoCH-only, and local-hour filters are
forbidden because those return strata were inspected in Phase 1.1. Order Blocks,
H1 support/resistance, fundamentals, EMA, RSI, and FVG are also excluded.

Phase 1.2 uses three sequential evidence locks:

1. construction coverage without access to returns;
2. construction P&L only for coverage-eligible filters; and
3. replication P&L for at most one construction winner frozen in a committed
   selection file.

No replication return may be produced if construction has no qualified winner.

## 3. Frozen signal source and execution

Every candidate starts from the causal full-session M15 BOS/CHoCH candidate
table generated by Phase 1.1. Each filter independently selects its first valid
candidate per London or New York session and may take at most one trade per
session.

Entry, quote side, commission, slippage, stop-first handling, `1 ATR` stop,
`2R` target, and session force-close are inherited unchanged from Phase 1.1.
There is no minimum time remaining before cutoff.

## 4. Preregistered light filters

| ID | Rules |
|---|---|
| F1 | displacement-qualified break |
| F2 | reject only explicit H1 opposition |
| F3 | reject only explicit H4 opposition |
| F4 | reject explicit opposition on either H1 or H4 |
| F5 | displacement plus H4 opposition veto |
| F6 | displacement plus H1 and H4 opposition vetoes |

Displacement is the already-frozen break-bar body threshold of at least
`0.80 ATR`; it is not retuned in this phase.

For a long, `bearish` is the only veto state. For a short, `bullish` is the only
veto state. All other states pass. The rule uses the latest state available at
the candidate decision timestamp.

## 5. Stage A — construction coverage only

Stage A reads candidate timestamps and causal feature labels but cannot read,
join, calculate, or write any return, price-exit, win, or P&L field.

A filter is coverage-eligible only when its 2024 first-valid selection produces
240–300 trades, corresponding to 20–25 trades per calendar month. Counts are
reported by session, direction, and month, but no subgroup can be removed.

If no filter is eligible, Phase 1.2 stops without strategy P&L.

## 6. Stage B — construction selection

Only coverage-eligible filters receive a 2024 full-cost simulation. P3 is the
fixed comparison. A filter qualifies for selection only when:

- mean trade net R is positive;
- profit factor exceeds one; and
- mean net R per common session-day opportunity exceeds P3.

At most one winner advances: the qualified filter with the highest construction
mean opportunity R. Exact ties use the fixed F1-to-F6 order. The winner ID,
construction metrics, input fingerprint, and definition must be committed to
`config/phase1_2_selection.yaml` before replication is opened.

If no filter qualifies, the run stops and 2025 filter P&L remains unopened.

## 7. Stage C — historical replication

Only the committed winner is simulated on 2025. It advances only if all frozen
conditions hold:

1. 240–300 replication trades;
2. positive mean trade and opportunity R;
3. profit factor above one;
4. better mean opportunity R than replication P3;
5. positive expectancy independently in London, New York, long, and short;
6. positive expectancy at 0.20-pip-per-side stress slippage;
7. the 95% day-cluster opportunity-mean CI is above zero; and
8. the best positive month supplies no more than 50% of total positive monthly
   R.

Passing historical replication still does not establish live readiness.

## 8. Invariants

- Stage A artifacts contain no return or execution-outcome columns.
- Selected signals are members of the parent causal candidate table.
- Feature availability cannot exceed decision time.
- Each model selects no more than one trade per session.
- Construction outputs contain no 2025 rows.
- Replication refuses to run without the committed selection file.
- Replication outputs contain only P3 and the single frozen winner.

Any invariant failure invalidates the stage independently of performance.
